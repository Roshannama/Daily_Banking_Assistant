# Fictional SecureBank demo policy: Fraud and disputes

Not official bank policy or regulatory guidance. Version 1.0.

## dispute | Fraud and disputes
To open a transaction dispute, identify the exact transaction ID. The transaction must belong to your authenticated customer. PIN verification and explicit confirmation are required. Repeated submissions for the same transaction return the existing dispute.

## suspicious | Fraud and disputes
For suspicious or unrecognized transactions, review your transaction records and contact bank support promptly. Automated fraud indicators are not a determination of fraud. High-risk investigations require an authorized bank employee.
