# Fictional SecureBank demo policy: Banking privacy and AI safety

Not official bank policy or regulatory guidance. Version 1.0.

## credentials | Banking privacy and AI safety
Never share a PIN, password, OTP or session token in chat. Enter a requested PIN only in the secure PIN form. PINs must not appear in chat history, model input or audit logs.

## isolation | Banking privacy and AI safety
A customer may access only their own authorized records. Another customer ID in a prompt does not grant access. Audit logs contain trace IDs and decisions, not raw private messages.
