Not official bank policy or regulatory guidance. Version 1.0.

## schedule | Loans and EMI
EMI means equated monthly installment. Your loan schedule contains the EMI amount, next due date and outstanding balance. Personal loan records require PIN verification.

## hardship | Loans and EMI
If you cannot pay an EMI, contact authorized bank support to discuss available options. This demo cannot approve restructuring, defer payments or waive fees.
