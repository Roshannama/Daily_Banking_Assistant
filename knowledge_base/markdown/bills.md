# Fictional SecureBank demo policy: Bill reminders and financial guidance

Not official bank policy or regulatory guidance. Version 1.0.

## reminders | Bill reminders and financial guidance
Upcoming payment reminders include recorded utility bills, subscriptions, credit card dues and EMIs. The demo displays reminders when asked; it does not send scheduled notifications or execute payments.

## saving | Bill reminders and financial guidance
Savings observations use successful income and expense records and upcoming obligations. Failed payments are not counted as settled spending. Remaining funds are an estimate excluding unrecorded future income or expenses. No investment return is guaranteed.
