# Fictional SecureBank demo policy: Debit and credit card services

Not official bank policy or regulatory guidance. Version 1.0.

## lost | Debit and credit card services
If a debit or credit card is lost or stolen, request card blocking promptly. This prototype requires the exact card ID, PIN verification and explicit confirmation before blocking. Use your bank emergency channel for urgent real incidents.

## replacement | Debit and credit card services
For card replacement, contact authorized bank support after protecting the lost card. Replacement fulfillment and fees are not implemented in this demo; no delivery date or fee is promised.
