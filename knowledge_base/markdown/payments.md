# Fictional SecureBank demo policy: UPI and payment failures

Not official bank policy or regulatory guidance. Version 1.0.

## failed | UPI and payment failures
For a failed UPI payment, check the transaction status and reference in your bank app. Avoid repeating the payment until its status is clear. If your account was debited, contact bank support with the reference. This fictional policy does not promise a refund deadline.

## pending | UPI and payment failures
A pending UPI payment has not reached a final status. Keep the reference number and check again in the bank app. Contact support if uncertainty persists. Do not assume pending means successful.

## definition | UPI and payment failures
UPI is a digital payment method used for bank-to-bank payments. Never share your payment PIN or OTP with a caller or in chatbot messages.
